Bridging Community

A flood-resilient urban design for the Windsor Bridge area on the Hawkesbury–Nepean River, where a 1-in-100-year flood would force about 64,000 people to evacuate. The proposal connects existing rooftops and vacant upper floors into a “bridging community”: shared food, art and culture spaces for everyday life that become vertical evacuation routes and shelter in a flood.

Urban context.
Three areas meet at the site: heritage and culture (orange), commercial (purple) and residential (blue).

Tourist and resident routes.
Visitors walk George Street to the heritage sites; residents head for the bridge and the supermarket.

Site constraints.
The southeast is cut off from the shops, one long block blocks walking through town, riverside homes sit apart, and the supermarket faces different uses on every side.

Flood extent.
Small floods reach the low ground. Half the site floods in a very large event, and all of it in a huge one.

Linking rooftops.
Roofs and vacant upper floors join into one network: daily life on top, vertical escape and bridges in large floods, and evacuation to South Windsor and Bligh Park in the biggest.

Shared common space.
Locals and visitors meet in the middle, in spaces that tie the neighbourhood together.

Private line, common space, public axis.
Common spaces link riverside homes (private line) to the shops on George Street (public axis).

Section and building types.
Homes, common space and the George Street axis sit against the 1-in-20, 1-in-100 and 1-in-500-year flood levels. Piloti buildings lift long-term uses; renovated buildings reuse upper floors.

Timeframe plan.
Start with a central common space, then grow the rooftop network out to distant homes.

Functions.
Food for locals, culture for visitors and art for both, with long-term uses on the roof and short-term uses below.

Sketches.
A gallery under the raised building and a farm on the roof bring daily life to the network.

No flood and large flood.
Every day the linked rooftops host food, art and culture. In a large flood the same network becomes an evacuation centre, operation base, food pantries and temporary housing.
